Australia Student Visa Consultant Perth: Complete Guide to Studying and Applying for a Student Visa in Australia

Published September 7, 2026 | Category: Student Visa

Studying in Australia can be an important academic and career decision. Students come to Australia for university degrees, vocational programs, English-language studies and other eligible courses, but choosing a course is only one part of the process.

International students must also understand admission requirements, Confirmation of Enrolment arrangements, visa requirements, financial planning, health insurance, Genuine Student requirements and visa conditions.

For students who want support throughout this process, working with an Australia Student Visa Consultant Perth can make the pathway easier to understand.

An experienced Australia Study Visa Consultant in Perth can help students consider both their immediate study plans and how their education fits with their longer-term career objectives.

Understanding the Australian Student Visa

The primary visa for international students is the Student visa subclass 500.

According to the Department of Home Affairs, the subclass 500 allows eligible students to undertake an eligible course in Australia and travel in and out of the country while the visa remains valid. The permitted stay depends on the student's enrolment and circumstances and may be granted for up to six years.

Student visa holders must comply with the conditions attached to their visa.

This is why applicants should not assume that receiving admission from an Australian education provider automatically guarantees a Student visa.

University or college admission and immigration approval are separate processes.

An effective student consultation should not begin with the question, "Which course is easiest?"

Instead, it should examine what course logically connects with the student's previous education, employment experience and future objectives.

Choosing the Right Course

Course selection is one of the most important parts of international study planning.

Students should consider:

  • Previous academic qualifications
  • Employment experience
  • Career goals
  • Course duration
  • Entry requirements
  • Tuition fees
  • Location
  • Education provider
  • Potential professional registration requirements
  • Future employment opportunities

Choosing a course simply because someone else selected it may create problems.

A student's academic progression should make sense when viewed against their overall background.

For example, someone with a bachelor's degree and several years of professional experience may need to clearly explain why a particular proposed course represents logical educational development.

This is where working with an Australia Study Visa Consultant in Perth can help students assess their study pathway before committing significant money and time.

Understanding the Genuine Student Requirement

The Genuine Student, or GS, requirement is an important component of the Student visa process.

It applies to Student visa applications lodged on or after 23 March 2024.

Home Affairs states that applicants must demonstrate that they are genuine applicants for entry and stay as students and that studying in Australia is the primary reason for applying for the Student visa.

The assessment may consider matters including:

  • Current circumstances
  • Family and community ties
  • Employment situation
  • Economic circumstances
  • Previous education
  • Reasons for selecting the course
  • Reasons for selecting Australia
  • Understanding of the course
  • Immigration history
  • How the proposed course benefits the applicant

Importantly, the GS framework recognises that a genuine international student may later become eligible for another visa or permanent residency. Future migration intentions alone do not automatically count against an applicant, provided studying is genuinely the primary purpose of the Student visa application.

Preparing Strong Genuine Student Responses

Student visa applicants answer specific GS questions as part of the online application.

Current Home Affairs guidance provides a 150-word limit for each primary GS response and encourages applicants to support statements with relevant evidence.

Good responses should be specific.

Instead of writing:

"I chose Australia because Australia has good education."

An applicant should explain:

  • Why the particular course was selected
  • Why that education provider was chosen
  • How the course relates to previous studies or employment
  • What skills the applicant expects to gain
  • How completing the qualification may help their career

Generic answers may fail to properly communicate the applicant's individual circumstances.

An Australia Student Visa Consultant Perth can help applicants understand what information is relevant without manufacturing or exaggerating facts.

All information supplied to immigration authorities should be truthful and supported where required.

Documents for an Australian Student Visa

The exact documentation required depends on individual circumstances.

Common documents may include:

  • Passport
  • Confirmation of Enrolment
  • Academic records
  • English-language evidence where required
  • Financial documents where required
  • Overseas Student Health Cover information
  • Employment documentation
  • Genuine Student supporting evidence
  • Relationship and family documents where applicable
  • Health or character-related documentation where required

Home Affairs provides a Student Visa Document Checklist Tool to help applicants determine what evidence may be required for their particular application.

Submitting a complete application is important. Home Affairs recommends checking documentation carefully and providing required evidence when lodging a Student visa application.

Student Visa Work Conditions

Many international students want to work while studying.

The current subclass 500 information states that most students can work up to 48 hours per fortnight while their course is in session. Different arrangements apply in specific circumstances, including certain postgraduate research students.

Students should check the exact conditions attached to their visa rather than relying on information from friends or older online articles.

Visa conditions can change, and breaching a visa condition may have serious consequences.

Student Visa Processing Priorities

Processing arrangements can also change.

For offshore Student visa applications lodged from 14 November 2025, Home Affairs currently applies Ministerial Direction 115, which uses three processing priority levels for relevant applications. The priority may depend partly on the education sector and provider circumstances. Home Affairs also makes clear that processing priority affects the order in which applications are considered, not whether the visa is ultimately granted or refused.

Students should therefore be cautious of anyone guaranteeing that a visa will be granted simply because a particular institution or processing category is involved.

What Happens After Completing Your Studies?

Study planning should also include consideration of what happens after graduation.

Some graduates may be eligible for a Temporary Graduate visa subclass 485, depending on the qualification completed and applicable eligibility criteria.

Home Affairs currently describes subclass 485 as a temporary visa that allows eligible Australian graduates to live, study and work in Australia for a period determined by their stream and qualification.

However, a graduate visa should not automatically be treated as a guaranteed permanent residency pathway.

Students who want to understand future possibilities may eventually need advice from an Australia PR Consultant Perth or Skilled Migration Consultant in Perth.

Future options may involve:

  • Skills assessment
  • Skilled Independent visa
  • State nomination
  • Regional migration
  • Employer sponsorship
  • Further eligible study
  • Other visa categories

Eligibility must be assessed under the rules applying at the relevant time.

Can Australian Study Lead to PR?

This is one of the most common questions asked of any Australia Immigration Consultant in Perth.

The correct answer is that Australian study does not automatically guarantee permanent residency.

A qualification may contribute to future eligibility depending on the applicant's circumstances, occupation, skills assessment, age, English ability, employment experience and migration program settings.

Applicants who eventually consider skilled migration may need to submit an Expression of Interest through SkillSelect for visas such as subclass 189, 190 or 491.

Accordingly, course selection should be based on genuine educational and career objectives rather than claims that a particular course guarantees PR.

Changing Courses or Extending a Student Visa

Some students discover that their circumstances change after arriving in Australia.

They may:

  • Need additional study time
  • Want to progress to a higher qualification
  • Consider changing providers
  • Change career direction
  • Have a visa approaching expiry

These situations should be reviewed carefully.

Changing education arrangements may have immigration consequences depending on the student's circumstances.

Before making a major change, obtaining advice from a Registered Migration Agent Perth may help the student understand the possible impact on their visa and future plans.
